norns-web

The script's real Lua, running in the browser against a stand-in for norns: the screen, encoders, keys, clocks and PARAMS are emulated, and the SuperCollider engine is replaced by rough Web Audio stand-ins. Good for checking behavior and the scene; the sound is only a sketch of the real engine.
